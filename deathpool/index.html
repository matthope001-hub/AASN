    HOW IT WORKS

    • Pick one celebrity from each category's box (two from Death Row).
    • When one of your picks dies, your cemetery scores that celeb's points, plus any bonuses below.
    • Mummy's Curse is the opposite: pick someone you do not want to die. If they die, you lose 10 points (B16).
    • Death Row: remember there are no guarantees they'll be put down.
    • Standings, deaths and points on this site come straight from the official pool sheet.
