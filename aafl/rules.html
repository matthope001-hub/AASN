RULES

2026 trial season · free · invite only · no prizes

🏈 THE GAME

  • Pick the winner of every NFL game, every week (regular season, Weeks 1–18).
  • 1 point for every correct pick. No point spreads.
  • A game that ends in a tie scores 0 for everyone. A game with no pick scores 0.

⏱️ PICK WINDOW

  • Picks for a week open as soon as the previous week kicks off (usually Thursday night), so you get a full week to pick.
  • The whole week locks at its first kickoff (usually Thursday night). After that, no picks can be added or changed.
  • You can change your picks as often as you like before the lock. Tap Submit picks to save.

👀 PICK REVEAL

  • Your picks are private until the week locks.
  • Once it locks, everyone's picks are shown on Standings → Weekly picks, and marked right or wrong as games finish.

🏆 STANDINGS

  • Season standings are total points. The weekly winner is whoever gets the most right that week.
  • No tiebreaker: tied teams share the spot. Tied for a top-3 place, they split that place's prize (no prizes this trial).
  • 1st, 2nd and 3rd are marked at the end of the season.

✉️ JOINING

  • The 2026 trial is invite only, up to 10 players, alongside 10 computer teams that pick at random.
  • Open your invite link and sign in with the email it was sent to, then name your team.
  • Joining mid-season? You get the league average for every week you missed, and start with the next week that hasn't kicked off.
  • Human players are marked (h) in the standings.
  • More than one entry? If your invite allows it, tap + Add entry at the top of the Picks page (up to the league limit per person). Each entry has its own picks; switch between them at the top of the Picks page.

💲 ENTRY FEE

  • The 2026 trial is free. Seasons with an entry fee show it on the Picks page as soon as you join.
  • Pay by Interac e-Transfer to the address shown, with AAFL and your team name(s) in the message. The fee is per team.
  • You can make picks right away, but a team's picks don't count in the standings until its fee is marked paid. Unpaid teams show $ unpaid.

📧 EMAIL REMINDERS

  • Tuesday morning: an email when the week's picks open. Thursday at noon: a reminder if you still have games to pick.
  • One email covers all your teams.
  • Turn them off: tap Unsubscribe at the bottom of any AAFL email, or untick Email me reminders at the top of the Picks page.
  • Turn them back on: sign in, open the Picks page and tick Email me reminders, or use the button on the unsubscribe page.

📊 SCORES & ODDS

  • Official scores and odds come from ESPN and update every 15 minutes (the score ticker is live).
  • Win chances on the pick cards come from the betting odds. They're a guide, not a guarantee.
