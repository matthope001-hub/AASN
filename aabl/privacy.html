PRIVACY

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • For each entry: your name, team name, email address and picks.
  • If you sign in with Google, Microsoft, Yahoo or an email link, we also keep the email address of that account so your teams show up when you sign in. We never see your password.
  • Whether your entry fee has been paid.

WHAT'S PUBLIC

  • Your team name and manager name appear in the standings.
  • Your picks and trades are hidden until picks lock on Opening Day, then everyone can see them.
  • Your email address is never shown on the site.

HOW IT'S USED

  • Email is only used for pool business: your entry confirmation, trade confirmations and a new team link if you ask for one. No newsletters, no ads.
  • Your information is never sold or shared. It's stored with our database provider (Supabase) and emails go out through Gmail.
  • The site doesn't use ad trackers. Your browser stores a few small settings (like whether you're signed in, or a draft of your entry) on your own device.

REMOVING YOUR INFO

  • Email info@theaahl.ca to have your information removed, or with any questions.
