POOL RULES

THE BASICS

  • Pick one player from each of the 21 boxes and the winner of all 6 MLB divisions — 27 picks in all.
  • Entry fee is $10 per team. Enter as many teams as you like.
  • Picks lock at the first pitch of MLB Opening Day.
  • Only the MLB regular season counts. Playoffs don't.

DIVISION WINNERS

  • Every correct division winner pick is worth +25 points.
  • The bonus is paid once, at the end of the regular season — it does not add up night by night. During the season the standings show a "projected" total using the current division leaders.

TWO-WAY PLAYERS

  • A two-way player (like Shohei Ohtani) can appear twice: once as a hitter and once as a pitcher, in different boxes.
  • The hitter version only scores hitting points; the pitcher version only scores pitching points. You can pick one, the other, or both.

TRADES

  • Before Opening Day: change any pick (and your division winners) as often as you like. Free.
  • During the season: 2 trades per team. Swap a player for another player in the same box.
  • Points the outgoing player earned for you are kept. The new player counts from the day of the trade.
  • Trades close at the MLB trade deadline. Division picks can't change once the season starts.
  • Make trades on the My Team page, using the private link in your confirmation email or your entry ID and email.
  • If a player is traded to another MLB team in real life, nothing changes in the pool — he stays on your team and keeps scoring.

PRIZES

  • The whole pot goes to the players — no 50/50 split.
  • Pot under $1,000: 🥇 1st 80% · 🥈 2nd 20%.
  • Pot $1,000 or more: 🥇 1st 50% · 🥈 2nd 40% · 🥉 3rd 10%.
  • Live payout amounts are on the Standings page.
  • Last place gets a free entry next season.
  • Late entries added by the commissioner score from day 1 of the season.
  • Tiebreakers (from your current picks, counting only while each player is on your team):
    1. Most home runs + RBI by your hitters.
    2. If still tied, most strikeouts by your pitchers.
    3. If still tied, the tied teams split the prize money for the places they cover.
